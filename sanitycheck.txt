RQ1.
What kind of parser/processor are LLMs?

Do they:

* wait for complete information?
* commit incrementally?
* revise?
* maintain uncertainty?

RQ2.
Do LLMs represent ambiguity?

When ambiguity appears:

* collapse immediately?
* maintain alternatives?
* revise later?

* preserve cue identity?
* compress into interpretation?
